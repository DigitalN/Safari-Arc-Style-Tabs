HOW TO INSTALL SIDE TABS

1. Drag "Side Tabs" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click "Side Tabs".
   macOS will say it can't verify the app. Click "Done".
   (Side Tabs is free and isn't sold through Apple, so Apple hasn't
   checked it. You only need to approve it once.)

3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the
   Security section, find "Side Tabs was blocked…", and click
   "Open Anyway". Enter your password, then click "Open Anyway" again.

4. A "Set Up Side Tabs" window appears. Follow its three steps:
   allow Accessibility, turn on the extension in Safari, and allow it
   on every website.

That's it: your tabs now appear in a sidebar next to Safari.

To uninstall: quit Side Tabs from its icon in the menu bar, then drag
it from Applications to the Trash.
